Welcome to Context Reader

Understands what you're reading, and explains it in your language.

1

Choose your language

Words will be explained in the language you pick. You can change it any time in Settings.

2

Add your free Gemini key

Context Reader has no server. Your browser asks Google’s Gemini directly, using a key that belongs to you — which is why it can be free permanently and has nothing to track you with. Getting a key takes about a minute and needs no card.

Get a key at Google AI Studio ↗

3

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece icon in your browser’s toolbar (top-right), find Context Reader, and pin it: the pin icon next to it, or in Firefox the gear, then Pin to Toolbar. That puts the language switch one click away.

4

Highlight a word you’re stuck on

Select it on any page. A small button appears next to your selection — click it.

5

Get the meaning this sentence needs

A dictionary gives every meaning of a word. Context Reader reads the sentence around it and gives you the one that fits — in the language you chose.

Free permanently. No tracking.

No account, no plan, no daily limit — there is no server of ours to pay for. The word you look up and the sentence around it go from your browser to Google’s Gemini with your key, and nowhere else. No analytics of any kind. The code is open source, so you can check.

One thing that is Google’s side, not ours: the free and paid tiers of the Gemini API differ in whether your prompts may be used to improve Google’s models. Which applies depends on the key you bring. The Gemini API terms say which, and our privacy policy says the same.
