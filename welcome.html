Welcome to Context Reader

Understands what you're reading, and explains it in your language.

1

Choose your language

Words will be explained in the language you pick. You can change it any time in Settings.

2

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece icon in your Chrome toolbar (top-right), find Context Reader, and click the pin icon next to it. This keeps it visible for quick access.

🧩 📌
3

Highlight any word

Select a word on any webpage by clicking and dragging over it.

👆 📝
4

Click the lightning bolt

A small icon appears near your selection. Click it to get the definition.

⚡
5

Get a context-aware definition

Unlike a dictionary, Context Reader gives you the meaning that fits what you're reading — in the language you chose.
