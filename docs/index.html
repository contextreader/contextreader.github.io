The company culture rewards long hours.

the shared habits and values of a workplace How people there are expected to behave, not their art or traditions.

A dictionary gives you both, plus the arts and customs of a society, and leaves the choosing to you.

How it works

Three steps. Only the first is yours.

  1. Select a word

    On any page. A small button appears next to it. Click it.

  2. It reads the sentence

    The word and the sentence around it go to Google’s Gemini, using your own key.

  3. You get the sense that fits

    Want more? More goes deeper, General shows the other meanings, and Simple puts it in plainer words.

Why it asks for your own key

No server in the middle. That’s the whole design.

Free, permanently

Nobody pays per lookup, so there’s no plan, no quota and no reason to start charging later.

No tracking

No analytics, telemetry or account. There’s nothing of ours for your reading to pass through, because there is nothing of ours.

Checkable, not promised

The code is open source under MIT. The extension holds exactly one host permission: Google’s Gemini API.

The cost, stated

You need a free Gemini key before your first lookup. It takes about a minute and no card.
