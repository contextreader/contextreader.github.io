How it works

  1. Select a word

    On any page. A small mark appears — click it.

  2. It reads the sentence

    The word and its surrounding context go to Google Gemini, using your own key.

  3. You get the sense that fits

    Plus deeper takes if you want them: a worked scenario, a dictionary entry, or a plain-language version.

Already in your language?

It won't translate a word into itself. Highlight a hard word in the language you already read, and it gives you a simpler one instead.

110 languages

Two are tuned — they ship worked examples, checked by a speaker, that teach the model to pick the sense matching the domain. The rest work, but without those examples domain-specific senses are less reliable. The extension says which is which instead of pretending they're equal.

Why it asks for your own key

There's no server

Your browser talks to Google directly. Nothing of yours passes through anything belonging to this project, because there is nothing to pass through.

So it stays free

Nobody is paying per-lookup costs, so there's no plan, no quota, and no reason to ever start charging.

And it's checkable

The extension may contact exactly one host, listed in its manifest and enforced by Chrome. You don't have to take our word for it.

The cost is honest: you need a free Gemini key before your first lookup. It takes about a minute.
